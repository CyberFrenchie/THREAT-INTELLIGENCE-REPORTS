## About

This site publishes open-source threat intelligence research by **Sam Dalgleish** (CyberFrenchie), a cybersecurity researcher and threat intelligence analyst based in Scotland.

My work focuses on OSINT-led investigation, vulnerability research, malware and infrastructure analysis, IOC validation, and responsible disclosure. I am a published CVE researcher (CVE-2025-9548), hold a BSc (Hons) in Digital Security & Forensics from Glasgow Caledonian University, and work as a SOC engineer at Acumen Cyber.

Everything published here is my own independent research, carried out in my own time. It is not conducted on behalf of my employer and does not draw on any client or employer data.

Reports are published in the public interest under TLP classification where appropriate and aim to provide clear, reproducible findings for defenders, researchers, and relevant authorities.
